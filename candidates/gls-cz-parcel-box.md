## Notes

- **Gap:** 2,059 GLS-owned lockers are in the feed. Only 471 have a GLS-tagged OSM locker within 40 m (Postpass CZ bbox, 2026-09-27), so **~1,590 GLS Parcel Boxes are missing**. Another ~1,000 have some other brand's locker within 40 m. Those are co-located lockers or mis-tagged ones.
- **Stable id:** `id` (for example `CZ10000-PARCELLOCK11`) is the ParcelShop ID that e-shops pass to GLS, so it is stable. `externalId` (`gls-36919`) and `goldId` are also present.
  - Propose `ref=<id>`, as for Z-BOX in Sync.
  - Today no OSM GLS locker has a ref.
- **Fields:** `type` (parcel-locker / parcel-shop), `name`, `contact.{postalCode,city,address}`, `location` [lat, lon], `hours` [[day, from, to]], `features` (acceptsCard, pickup, delivery …), `hasWheelchairAccess`, `parcelLockerExternalProvider`.
- **Other GLS endpoints:** the same data is served through the map's internal `https://maps.gls-czech.cz/ajax.php` (action getList, 5,547 items). Prefer the static JSON.
- **Tagging:**
  - Wiki read: Tag:amenity=parcel_locker (raw). Recommended keys are brand (important), operator, ref, parcel_mail_in and opening_hours. Do not put the locker id into `name`.
  - NSI checked: data/brands/amenity/parcel_locker.json.
- **Coverage:** not in ATP. There is no GLS CZ spider; `general_logistics_systems_de` covers DE only.
- **Licence:** none published, so consent is needed. Zásilkovna consent (Cs:Česko/freemap) is the precedent. Contact: info@gls-czech.com.
